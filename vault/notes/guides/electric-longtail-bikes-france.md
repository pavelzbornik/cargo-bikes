## Electric Longtail Cargo Bikes in the French Market

A market overview of electric [[Cargo Bike Terminology|longtail]] cargo bikes available in France, compiled from research across 28 models spanning budget to premium segments.

## Market by Price Segment

### Budget (€2,200–€3,500)
- **Unikride Longtail** — €2,200, basic specs, good value
- **Decathlon BTwin R500E** — €2,699, the most popular budget option with solid reviews
- **Yuba Kombi E5** — €2,499–€2,999, entry-level from an established brand
- **Gaya Cargo** — €2,700, compact French-made option

### Mid-Range (€3,500–€5,000)
- **AddBike U-Cargo Family** — €3,490, Lyon-made, strong build quality
- **Yuba Mundo Circular** — €3,499, high capacity (250 kg) with sustainable components
- **O2feel Equo Cargo Boost 3.1** — €3,699–€3,999, Lille brand with reliable Shimano motors
- **Bergamont E-Cargoville LT Edition** — €4,500, German engineering with Bosch systems

### Premium (€5,000+)
- **Yuba Spicy Curry V4** — €5,849–€5,999, Bosch Cargo Line, carries up to 3 children
- **Etni Cycles Oléron** — €5,589–€5,600, artisanal French (Nantes)
- **Tern GSD R14** — €10,200, dual-battery capability, ABS braking
- **Riese & Müller Load Series** — €8,500–€10,000, German luxury with full suspension

## Specifications at a Glance

- **Length**: 176 cm (Le Petit Porteur) to 220 cm (Yuba Mundo Circular) — storage space matters.
- **Weight**: 26 kg (Gaya Cargo) to 44 kg (AddBike U-Cargo Family); most sit at 35–45 kg, which makes manual handling a real consideration.
- **Max load**: 160 kg up to 250 kg (Yuba Mundo Circular).
- **Motors**: Bosch Cargo Line (85 Nm) and Shimano EP6/EP8 Cargo (85 Nm) on premium/mid-range; Shimano E6100 (60 Nm) and Bafang on budget/French-made models. See [[E-Bike Motors Explained]].
- **Battery / range**: 400 Wh to 1,600 Wh (dual battery); 60 km to 288 km depending on capacity and use.

## French Manufacturers

Douze Cycles (Burgundy), O2feel (Lille), AddBike (Lyon), Etni Cycles (Nantes), and Gaya all build locally, with prices roughly €2,700–€5,600. International brands with strong French distribution include Tern, Yuba, and Riese & Müller.

## Recommendations by Use Case

- **Family (2+ children)**: Yuba Spicy Curry V4, Tern GSD S10, AddBike U-Cargo Family
- **Budget-conscious**: Decathlon BTwin R500E, Yuba Kombi E5, Gaya Cargo
- **Professional / heavy use**: Tern GSD R14, Riese & Müller Load, Etni Cycles Oléron
- **Urban commuting**: O2feel Equo Cargo, Bergamont E-Cargoville, Moustache Lundi 20

## Common Concerns

Weight management (35–45 kg), storage for 200–220 cm length, hill performance of rear-hub motors versus mid-drive, and the price-versus-features tradeoff at the premium end.
